    Questions

    Before you begin.

    Is Birth Code only astrology?

    No. Astrology is one of ten systems. You will also find numerology, the Pythagoras square, the secret of your name, BaZi, Feng Shui, Chinese astrology, tarot with the Destiny Matrix, angel numbers and your life cycles.

    How exact are the calculations?

    Your chart comes from real planetary positions for your time and place. BaZi uses true solar time and the solar terms. Every method is named in the app, and every calculation is shown so you can check it.

    Do I need my birth time?

    It helps. Without it you still get most of your code; only the parts that depend on the hour, such as your Ascendant, your houses and your BaZi hour pillar, are left out rather than guessed.

    Is it predicting my future?

    No. Birth Code treats these traditions as symbolic languages for self-reflection, not as fortune-telling. It never tells you what will happen; it helps you understand what is already there.

    Where is my data kept?

    Your birth details are stored on your phone. Birth Code does not need an account to read your code.

    Which languages is it in?

    English and Russian, fully written in both, and you can switch at the top of any page.

    Can I read my partner or my friends too?

    Yes. Save the people who matter to you and read your compatibility, from simple element harmony to the real contacts between your two charts.

    Why do different apps give different results?

    Because schools use different conventions: house systems, the day boundary in BaZi, how numbers are reduced. Birth Code chooses one convention for each system, names it, and uses it the same way for everyone.

    Is it only for women?

    No. Birth Code has a women's edition and a men's edition, each written in full, with its own tone and its own style guidance. You choose yours with your birth details and can change it at any time.
